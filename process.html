Step-by-step

How your website project moves forward.

Each stage has a clear purpose so you understand what is happening, what is needed, and what comes next.

STEP 01

Apply

Complete the website application and share information about your business, goals, audience, current website, and the type of support you need.

STEP 02

Discovery

We review your application, discuss your business, clarify your goals, and determine whether PopTheWeb is the right fit for your project.

STEP 03

Project Recommendation

PopTheWeb recommends the website service and solution that best match your current needs, business stage, and desired outcome.

STEP 04

Planning

The pages, content, features, integrations, responsibilities, timeline, and project scope are organized before design work begins.

STEP 05

Content Preparation

You provide the approved business information, written content, photographs, branding materials, service details, and other required project assets.

STEP 06

Design

The website layout, visual direction, colors, typography, structure, and customer experience are developed around your business and audience.

STEP 07

Development

The approved design is transformed into a responsive, functional website with the agreed pages, features, forms, and integrations.

STEP 08

Review and Revisions

You review the website and provide organized feedback. Approved revisions are completed according to the project scope.

STEP 09

Testing

The website is checked across screen sizes, links, forms, navigation, content, accessibility, and major browser experiences.

STEP 10

Launch

After final approval, the website is connected to the selected domain and hosting platform and prepared for public visitors.

STEP 11

Handoff

You receive the agreed project information, account guidance, and instructions needed to understand the completed website.

STEP 12

Continued Support

Ongoing website support, updates, redesign work, and business automation services may be added based on your future needs.

What keeps the process moving

A successful project depends on clear participation.

PopTheWeb provides the structure and guidance, while the client provides timely information, decisions, content, and feedback.

01

Timely Communication

Responding to questions and reviewing project updates on time helps prevent unnecessary delays.

02

Complete Content

Business details, written content, images, branding, and account information should be provided before the related work begins.

03

Organized Feedback

Clear, complete feedback helps revisions move efficiently and keeps the project aligned with the approved scope.

Before the project begins

Important project responsibilities.

These responsibilities help protect your business ownership and keep the website organized after launch.

01

Domain and Hosting

Clients are responsible for purchasing and owning their domain name, hosting account, and required third-party services.

02

Business Content

Clients are responsible for providing accurate business information, approved written content, images, policies, and legal information.

03

Final Approval

The client reviews and approves the website before launch and confirms that the information presented is accurate.
