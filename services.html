How to Select Your Project

Your service determines the work. Your solution determines the outcome.

These two choices work together to create a project that fits both the condition of your website and what your business needs the finished website to accomplish.

STEP 01

Select the Website Service

Choose Custom Website Design when starting fresh, Website Redesign when improving an existing site, or Website Support when you need help maintaining and managing a website you already have.

STEP 02

Select the Business Outcome

Choose Brand Presence to establish credibility, Lead Generation to increase inquiries, or Business Automation to connect your website with systems that handle repeated tasks.

STEP 03

Define the Project Scope

The required pages, content, integrations, third-party tools, timeline, and responsibilities are identified before the project begins so both sides understand what is included.

STEP 04

Receive a Project Recommendation

After reviewing your needs, PopTheWeb recommends the service and solution combination that best fits your current stage instead of adding features your business does not need.
